# Fivefold Veil: recording plan

Record at 1920×1080 or 1280×720, 30 fps. Capture only Chrome. Turn microphone off; narration will be added afterward. Hide notifications and the Edit site badge. Keep the pointer visible. Pause two seconds before and after each action. Record separate clips if easier; send the original MP4s without background music.

## Clip 1 — Portal and host (30–40 seconds)
Show the portal for three seconds. Name a new expedition, choose Small crew, expand Control mode & timer, and show the three modes. Select Shared control and 12 minutes. Create the room. Show Invite team, then close the invite dialog. Never show tokens or private host keys.

Voice: “Fivefold Veil turns a stream audience into a research team. Create a room, choose how much control to share, and invite your viewers. The host directs the pace while the audience investigates together.”

## Clip 2 — Join and experiment (40–50 seconds)
Open a viewer seat in a second tab. Join as Demo Viewer. Return to the host tab and click Begin encounter. Return to the viewer, scroll to the emoji keyboard, choose a color, shape and pulse count, and send the experiment. Hold on the actual journal result. Show the remaining theories and cast an eligible vote. Do not stage a successful result or claim a browser command came from Twitch.

Voice: “Viewers build signals with a simple emoji keyboard. Each experiment adds evidence to a shared journal. Even a failed signal helps eliminate theories. Contributions earn a voice in the team's decision.”

## Clip 3 — Guided participation (20–30 seconds)
Open Guided mode. Click OK Next twice, show the arrow and highlighted control, then Skip. Toggle Readable mode on, pause, and switch it off.

Voice: “New participants can follow guided steps. The guide explains each control without submitting actions for them. Readable mode adds clarity, while decorative effects remain optional.”

## Clip 4 — Domain (20–30 seconds)
Open the domain chooser. Preview two domains, then choose Lunar. Enter a domain room, select a landmark, and show its relay preview. Open the lounge and briefly show the scene and chat controls. Keep audio off for the voiceover edit.

Voice: “Six original domains give teams a shared home. Explore linked landmarks, recover memories, and gather in a domain lounge between discoveries.”

## Competition evidence clips (20–30 seconds total)
Record a real Twitch chat command and its matching receipt in the app. Separately record an actual Codex development session showing a real change or test. Keep credentials hidden. These clips are required for the competition demo; browser-only footage does not establish Twitch integration.

Voice: “A real Twitch chat command enters the same investigation. Built with Codex, Fivefold Veil connects streamer control, shared evidence, and guided participation. Explore apart. Think together.”

Aim for a final edit under three minutes. Capture more raw footage if needed; idle time can be trimmed. Do not submit until judge access and the official submission checklist are complete.

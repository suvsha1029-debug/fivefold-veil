      FIVEFOLD VEIL / FIELD GUIDE

      Five discoveries.
      One shared signal.

      The guardian has five eyes. Each follows a different hidden rule, and every eye is necessary to identify the single signal that opens the veil.

      1. 01
        Investigate an eye.

        Pick Crown, Dusk, Heart, Dawn, or Root. Send !test crown blue circle 2. Each eye has six unique experiments. Each viewer may test an eye twice in 30 seconds, so spread the investigation.

      2. 02
        Let evidence earn a voice.

        After contributing a test at an eye, vote with !solve crown a (a–d). Your latest vote replaces your previous one. The streamer seals the leading theory; ties need agreement.

      3. 03
        Bind all five discoveries.

        After sealing every eye, use the five revealed rules to find one common signal. Contributors propose it with !bind blue circle 2, then the streamer commits the leading proposal.

      Three incorrect seals or bindings fracture the veil. A case code always generates the same puzzle. Recovery resumes paused; Twitch sign-in is kept only in memory. Practice contributors are simulations.

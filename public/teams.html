    THE EXPEDITION FIELD GUIDE

    Five teams.
    One shared signal.

    The streamer chooses how to share control. Viewers lead lets evidence contributors confirm leading votes. Shared control reserves confirmation for the host. Host-led queues experiments for approval and lets the host choose answers; viewer votes are advisory. The mode banner always explains the current rules. Hosts can change mode while the clock is held. The puzzle editor is available only before a round starts.

    Watching is welcome. Join with a name to contribute; team and emblem choices are optional. Ask the host for a nudge when stuck. A host-shared nudge suggests how to compare the public theories; it never runs an experiment or earns a vote for you.

    Join a room and choose a team. Your team has a home eye, and everyone may help investigate any eye. The shared journal and discoveries belong to the whole expedition.

    Use the emoji keyboard to compose colour, shape and pulses. Arrow keys move within a key group. Ctrl/Cmd + Enter sends. Text commands and emoji commands are both accepted in Twitch chat.

    Each eye has six unique experiments. You may test it twice every 30 seconds. You can also earn voting eligibility by identifying an observation that contradicts a displayed theory. This does not spend an experiment.

    Vote for your team’s theory. The current control mode determines who confirms the theory. After five seals, find one signal satisfying every rule and propose a binding. Team expedition needs evidence from two teams; small crew needs one. The host chooses this when creating the room. Three wrong seals/bindings end the encounter.

    Your room code is public. Team IDs combine that code and the team name. Your member ID identifies this browser seat; it is not proof of a unique person or a Twitch login. Refresh keeps the seat in this tab. Your team becomes fixed after contributing, until a new round. Teams choose a home domain by voting: one replaceable vote per member. An outright leading domain becomes the home; ties keep the current one. Homes persist across new rounds. Previews only change your own browser until you vote.

    Enter a domain room to visit its three landmarks. Each domain has its own relay rule. Inspect the affected landmarks and predicted phases before tuning. Match all three targets to recover a room memory. All teammates share progress, including across refreshes and visits to other domains. New rounds reset the rooms. This optional relay does not spend experiments or change the guardian’s rules. Tuning also fixes your research team for the round.

    Twitch relay commands: !team crown, !domain corrupted, !tune 1, !test crown 🔵 ⚪ 2️⃣, !cite crown 2 b, !solve crown a, !bind blue circle 2. In Viewers lead mode, eligible contributors can use !seal crown and !confirmbind. The host connects their own channel. Browser guests and Twitch relay participants are separate identities.
